Welcome to the official Employee Leave Request portal of BrightFuture Tech Solutions.
This page has been created to simplify the process of applying for leave by replacing manual paperwork with a structured online form.
Employees can now submit their leave requests quickly and efficiently, ensuring transparency and proper record-keeping.
Please provide accurate details so that your request can be processed without delays.

The form collects essential information such as your employee ID, department, leave type, and duration.
You will also be asked to specify whether you can be reached during your leave and provide an alternate contact number.
Supporting documents can be uploaded if required, and a declaration must be accepted before submission.
Kindly ensure that all mandatory fields are completed to avoid rejection of your request.
