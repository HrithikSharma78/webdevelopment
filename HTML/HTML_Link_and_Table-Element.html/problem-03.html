Faculty Information

The department has a team of highly qualified faculty members who are dedicated to teaching and research. Each faculty member brings knowledge and experience to the classroom, ensuring quality education. They also mentor students and guide them in their academic and professional journeys. Their commitment to excellence creates a vibrant and supportive learning environment.

Academic Rules

  • Attendance is mandatory for all classes.
  • Assignments must be submitted before the deadline.
  • Respect and discipline must be maintained in classrooms.

Important Notice: Internal assessments will be conducted in the third week of every month.

Sequence Before Practical Class

  1. Review the lab manual and understand the experiment.
  2. Prepare the required materials and tools in advance.
  3. Discuss the procedure with your lab partner before starting.

Definitions

Lecture
A formal teaching session where concepts are explained in detail.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments and gain hands-on experience.
Tutorial
A small group session focused on problem-solving and discussion.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted within the department to measure student progress.
Practical
Application-based learning where theory is implemented in real scenarios.
