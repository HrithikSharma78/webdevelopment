Annual College Fest 2026

Fest Introduction

Welcome to the Annual College Fest 2026, the most awaited event of the year. This fest brings together students from all departments to showcase their talents and creativity. The event includes competitions, cultural programs, workshops, and entertainment sessions. Join us for three days of fun, learning, and unforgettable experiences.

Registration Process

  1. Visit the official fest registration portal.
  2. Fill in your personal and academic details.
  3. Select the events you wish to participate in.
  4. Upload required documents and photographs.
  5. Pay the registration fee online.
  6. Submit the form before 5th October 2026.

Rules

  • All participants must carry their college ID card.
  • Respect and discipline must be maintained during events.
  • Teams must report 30 minutes before their scheduled event.
  • Decisions of judges will be final.

Definitions

Open Event
Events open to all students without restrictions.
Team Event
Competitions requiring group participation.
Individual Event
Competitions where students participate alone.
Registration
Formal process of enrolling for the fest.
Coordinator
Faculty or staff responsible for managing an event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where winners are decided.
