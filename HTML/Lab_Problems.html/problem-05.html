Quantum Computing Basics

Quantum computing introduces concepts like qubits and superposition.
Students are learning how 2 states can exist simultaneously, unlike
classical bits. Research labs are experimenting with low‑temperature
 environments to stabilize qubits. These advancements promise faster
problem‑solving capabilities.
