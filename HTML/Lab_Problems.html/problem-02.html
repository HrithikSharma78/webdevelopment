Featured Story: Annual Tech Fest 2026

Innovation Meets Creativity

The Annual Tech Fest 2026 was inaugurated with great enthusiasm at GLA University. Students from multiple departments showcased their projects and ideas. The most important highlight was the AI‑powered EV assistant prototype developed by the Computer Science team.

The fest included workshops, coding competitions, and exhibitions. Participants emphasized the importance of collaboration and innovation. Faculty members encouraged students to think beyond textbooks and apply knowledge practically.

A record number of 1,200 students participated this year, making it the largest tech fest in the university’s history. On 28th August 2026, the fest concluded with an award ceremony recognizing top innovators.

The event also attracted industry experts who shared insights on emerging technologies. Their presence motivated students to pursue research and entrepreneurship. The fest served as a bridge between academia and industry.
