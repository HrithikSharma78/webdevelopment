Application Process

  1. Register online at the official admission portal.
  2. Fill in personal and academic details.
  3. Upload required documents.
  4. Pay the application fee.
  5. Submit the application before 30th September 2026.
  6. Download the confirmation slip.

Required Documents

  • High school mark sheet
  • Transfer certificate
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Government-issued ID proof

Important Terms

Eligibility
Minimum academic qualifications required for admission.
Merit List
Ranked list of applicants based on performance.
Counselling
Process of seat allocation and guidance.
Scholarship
Financial aid awarded to deserving students.
Registration
Formal enrollment into the university program.
