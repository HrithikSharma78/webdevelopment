Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

All students must carry their ID card while on campus.

Respect for faculty, staff, and peers is expected at all times.

Attendance

Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory to sit for examinations.

Failure to meet attendance requirements will result in disqualification.

Examination

Cheating or unfair means will lead to immediate cancellation of the exam.

Students must arrive at least 15 minutes before the scheduled time.

Assignment Submission

Assignments must be submitted before the deadline.
Late submissions will incur a 10% penalty per day.

Discipline

Use of mobile phones in classrooms is strictly prohibited.

Students are expected to maintain decorum in labs and libraries.

Important Notice

Final project submissions close on 30th September.
No extensions will be granted.
